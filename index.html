I'm John Huang (Huang Tung Yee John) — an undergraduate in Mathematics with an Extended Major in Artificial Intelligence at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Class of 2029). This site collects my work, notes, and the things I'm learning along the way.

Most recently I spent a summer at the Academy of Mathematics and System Science, Chinese Academy of Sciences, where I helped develop and validate the CAX integrated algorithm platform — turning mathematical thinking into software that runs. Before that, my background was mostly competition mathematics: an ICAS Principal's Award and High Distinction, and a Bronze in the AIMO senior-secondary semi-final. You can find the fuller list on the About page.

Beyond coursework, I've chased mathematics and science wherever they led — a summer class on differential geometry, the China Aerospace Education Program, and the Astronomical Training Programme for secondary students. I write in Life & Notes about what I'm reading, thinking, and trying out.

I work comfortably in C and Python, and I enjoy helping other students find their footing through the HKUST Science Peer Mentor Program. If you'd like to talk — maths, code, or anything in between — the easiest way is the Contact page.
